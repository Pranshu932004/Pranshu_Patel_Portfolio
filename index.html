About

From backend code to boardroom insight

Pranshu started out writing Laravel backend systems — RESTful APIs, optimized MySQL schemas and the kind of complex SQL queries that make reporting fast and reliable. That backend discipline now shapes how he approaches data: structured, validated, and built to be trusted.

Between October 2025 and June 2026, he served as the primary data resource at Kotak Mahindra Bank for regions spanning Gujarat, Delhi, Rajasthan, Punjab, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and South India — turning large-scale regional business data into MIS reports, performance trackers and executive-ready presentations that leadership teams acted on.

He holds a B.Sc in Computer Science & Information Technology from Silver Oak University (CGPA 8.44/10) and is currently in his second semester of an M.Sc in Data Science — pairing the engineering fundamentals with formal analytical training.
